What the chair is for

Services named on public listings and in reviews. Prices are set in the shop — call or text before you come. There is no public price list.

  • Haircut

    The reason most people sit down. Guests talk about cuts that match what they asked for, including kids.

  • Fades & line-ups

    Listed among the shop’s regular work. Ask for the shape you want when you call.

  • Beard work

    Beard grooming alongside the cut, the way reviewers describe a full visit.

  • Shave

    Shaves are part of the published service list.

  • Eyebrows

    Eyebrow grooming is mentioned with the rest of the chair work.

  • Shampoo

    Offered with the cut. Confirm it when you book the time.

Appointments are recommended. Reviews say the shop is mostly by appointment, and that a walk-in is sometimes worked in.
